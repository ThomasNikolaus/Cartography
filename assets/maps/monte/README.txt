Urbano Monte — 60 numbered map sheets (1587)

karte-01.webp through karte-60.webp follow the original map sheet numbers.
Each WebP is at most 400 pixels wide, suitable for the small illustrations
beside the Exploration Chart PDF links (including high-density screens).
Use the catalogue links in manifest.json to obtain larger source images.

The four illustrations already used by the Cartography website are:
01 = There is no infinite Künneth (yet)
11 = Group completion commutes with infinite products
40 = The immersion conjecture
55 = The Segal Conjecture

Sources, catalogue numbers, dimensions and compression details are recorded
in manifest.json. The two cover scans, diagrams and the duplicate scan of
sheet XLII (10130.045, portrait lifted) are not counted among the 60 sheets.
Sheet 42 uses catalogue record 10130.044.
